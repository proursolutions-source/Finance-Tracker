Cookie Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

In short: MoneyFlow sets one first-party cookie for a consent preference, plus browser storage (localStorage/IndexedDB) that never leaves your device. No advertising or tracking cookies are used.

What we use

NamePurposeTypeDuration
moneyflow_consentRemembers your analytics consent choiceFirst-party cookieUntil cleared
moneyflow-pin-hash, entitlement cache, theme, etc.App preferences and your own device PIN locklocalStorageUntil cleared
Supabase auth sessionKeeps you signed in to MoneyFlow Cloud (subscription/admin features only)localStorageUntil sign-out or expiry
Your financial data (transactions, budgets, etc.)The app's local databaseIndexedDBUntil you clear it or export/delete

What we don't use

No third-party advertising cookies, no cross-site tracking pixels, and no cookie is ever used to identify you across other websites.

Managing your choice

You can change your analytics consent at any time from Settings, or clear all site data from your browser's own settings — this will also remove your local financial data, so export it first if you want to keep it.
