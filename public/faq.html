Frequently Asked Questions

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Is my financial data private?

Yes. Your transactions, budgets, and accounts are stored only on your own device (in your browser's IndexedDB). MoneyFlow doesn't operate a server that receives this data. See the Privacy Policy.

What happens if I clear my browser data or switch devices?

Since your financial data lives only on this device, clearing site data or switching devices means it won't be there. Export a backup regularly from Settings → Export Data.

Do I need an account to use MoneyFlow?

No — a local device PIN/password is enough for the core app. A MoneyFlow Cloud account (email or Google) is only needed for Subscription and Admin features.

Why do I see a lock icon next to some features?

Those are Pro or Premium features, gated by your subscription tier. See the Subscription page to view or change plans.

How do I pay for a subscription?

Via a direct UPI payment shown as a QR code at checkout — no card details or payment gateway involved. After paying, submit your reference number and an admin will verify and activate your plan.

Can I get a refund?

Since payments are manual and peer-to-peer (not through a payment gateway), refunds are handled directly — use the Feedback/Support form in the app to reach out.

How do I delete my account?

From Settings, use "Request Account Deletion". Since this is manually processed, it may take a short time for an admin to complete it.

Still stuck? Use the Feedback form in the app, or see Disclaimers.
