Disclaimers

Last updated: 27 September 2026

In short: MoneyFlow is a personal record-keeping and planning tool. It does not give financial, investment, tax, or legal advice, and its calculators and AI-adjacent features are estimates only.

Financial disclaimer

MoneyFlow helps you track and organize your own financial information. Nothing in the app — budgets, goals, reports, or summaries — constitutes financial advice or a recommendation to take any particular action with your money. Decisions you make based on information in the app are your own responsibility.

Investment disclaimer

The Investments feature is a manual record of transactions you enter yourself against your own account valuations — it does not pull live market prices, and figures shown (current value, gain/loss) are only as accurate as the values you keep up to date. MoneyFlow does not recommend, endorse, or advise on any security, fund, or asset.

Tax disclaimer

MoneyFlow does not calculate or file taxes and is not a substitute for advice from a qualified tax professional or your local tax authority. Any tax-related categorization in the app (e.g. category names) is for your own organizational convenience only.

Loan & interest calculators

The EMI, interest, amortization, and debt payoff planner tools use standard formulas for estimation purposes only. Actual figures from your bank or lender (which may include fees, rounding, or compounding differences) will take precedence over anything calculated here.

AI / automated-feature disclaimer

Where MoneyFlow uses automated processing (e.g. OCR bill scanning), results are estimates that you should always verify before relying on them — the app deliberately asks you to confirm scanned amounts before saving, for exactly this reason.
