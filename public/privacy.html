Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

In short: your financial data (transactions, budgets, accounts, goals) is stored only in your browser's local storage on your own device. It is never uploaded to us. The only things that ever leave your device are, if you turn them on: anonymous product-usage analytics, and OCR bill-scanning requests to a third-party model provider. This page explains both in detail.

1. Data you enter

Transactions, categories, budgets, reminders, goals, accounts, lending/debt records, and your profile are stored locally on your device (via IndexedDB) and are not transmitted to any MoneyFlow server, because MoneyFlow does not operate one. Exporting your data (Settings → Export Data) creates a file on your own device that only you control.

2. Local account (Login/Signup)

The name, email, and password you enter at signup are used only to gate access to the app on this device. Your password is hashed before being stored locally; the email address is never sent anywhere and is not used to contact you.

3. Cookies

MoneyFlow sets a single first-party cookie, moneyflow_consent, to remember your analytics consent choice. It contains no personal data — just "accepted" or "declined". No third-party or advertising cookies are set.

4. Analytics (optional, off until you consent)

If you accept the cookie/analytics banner, MoneyFlow records anonymous, aggregate product-usage events (e.g. "a page was viewed") to help improve the app. These events do not include your financial data, name, or email. You can withdraw consent at any time from Settings, which stops all future analytics events. If analytics is not configured for your build of the app, no data is collected even if you consent.

5. Bill scanning (OCR)

The "Scan Bill" feature processes a photo of a receipt using an on-device/browser OCR engine (Tesseract.js) to guess an amount and date. The recognition model is downloaded from a CDN the first time you use this feature, but the receipt photo itself is processed locally and is not uploaded anywhere.

6. Data deletion

You can permanently delete all locally stored data at any time from Settings → Reset All Data. Because nothing is stored on a server, there is nothing further for us to delete on our end.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change. Since the app has no way to contact you, please check this page periodically.

8. Contact

This is a template privacy policy for an offline-first personal project, not reviewed by a lawyer. Replace this section with real contact details if you deploy MoneyFlow for other people to use.
