Day to Day

Dashboard

Your financial overview at a glance: total balance across all accounts, this month's income and expense, and your most recent transactions.

Open Dashboard

Transactions

Every income and expense you've logged, with tools to find and manage them:

  • Search by payee or notes, filter by type, category, or date range
  • Edit or delete any entry — deleting shows an Undo for a few seconds
  • Attach a receipt photo, or link a transaction to a Net Worth account
Open Transactions

Scan Bill

From the Add Transaction form, tap Scan to photograph a paper bill. On-device OCR guesses the total amount and date and fills them in — always shown as a suggestion to check, never applied silently. The photo itself is also attached as the transaction's receipt.

Try it from Transactions

Split Bill

Splitting a bill with other people: your own share is recorded as a normal expense, and everyone else's share is automatically added to the Lending & Debt page as money they owe you (or, if someone else paid, as money you owe them).

Open Split Bill

Categories

Create, edit, or remove income/expense categories with a custom name, icon, and color, beyond the built-in defaults.

Open Categories

Planning

Budgets

Set a spending limit for a category (weekly, monthly, or yearly) and track actual spend against it with a progress bar that changes color as you approach the limit.

Open Budgets

Reports

Analytics across your whole financial history:

  • Projection vs Actual — combines your budgets and recurring bills into a monthly plan, compared to what you've actually spent so far
  • Income vs expense trend (last 12 months), spending by category, budget adherence, savings rate, and a 3-month forecast
Open Reports

Reminders

One-off or recurring bill reminders with an amount and due date. Mark one as paid when you've settled it, and get an optional notification when it's due (see Settings).

Open Reminders

Goals

Set a savings target with a deadline and priority, then log contributions toward it and watch the progress bar fill in.

Open Goals

Memory

The story behind your money, not just the numbers:

  • Attach an optional memory (a title and short story) to any transaction from the Add/Edit Transaction form
  • Financial Milestones (first ₹1,000/10,000/50,000/1,00,000 saved, first salary, first investment, first debt cleared) are detected automatically from your transaction history
  • On This Day surfaces a memory or your spend from the same date in a previous year, on the Dashboard and the Memory page
  • The Memory Timeline combines memories, milestones, and manually-added life events (from before you started using MoneyFlow) into one chronological view
Open Memory

Accounts & Debt

Net Worth

Every account you own in one place — savings, FDs, PPF, mutual funds, stocks, gold, property, credit cards, and loans:

  • Assets and liabilities are totalled automatically into your net worth, with a trend chart over time
  • Credit cards show a utilization bar against their limit; loans show the EMI amount and next due date
  • Pay Bill / Pay EMI on a card or loan opens a pre-filled transfer from one of your other accounts
Open Net Worth

Transfers

Move money between two of your own accounts — e.g. bank to credit card, or bank to loan. Transfers update both balances but are never counted as income or expense, and appear in a Recent Transfers log on the Net Worth page.

Open Net Worth

Recurring

Bills and income that repeat on a schedule (daily to yearly) — rent, salary, subscriptions. Process Due turns any that are due into real transactions in one tap.

Open Recurring

Lending & Debt

Money you've lent to someone (they owe you) or borrowed (you owe them), tracked per person with partial repayments — an entry settles itself automatically once fully paid.

Open Lending & Debt

Account & App

Settings

Everything about your profile and your data:

  • Edit your profile, toggle light/dark theme, log out
  • Turn on notifications for due reminders and budget overspend
  • Set an app PIN lock for quick re-entry on this device
  • Export all your data as CSV or JSON, or import a CSV/JSON backup back in
  • Reset all local data, or manage your cookie/analytics consent
Open Settings

Login & Security

MoneyFlow creates a local account on first use (name, email, password) and asks for it again each time you open the app on this device. There is no server and no password recovery — forgetting your password means resetting the local account. An optional PIN adds a faster second unlock step. Both lock out for 30 seconds after 5 wrong attempts.

Subscription

MoneyFlow's paid plans, managed through a separate MoneyFlow Cloud account (not your local device login). View plans, subscribe, apply a discount code, or cancel. No live payment gateway is connected yet — subscribing records your intent, and an admin activates it manually in the meantime. Requires a one-time setup by whoever runs this MoneyFlow instance (see SETUP_SUPABASE.md); until then this page explains what's missing instead of failing silently.

Open Subscription

Admin Portal

Visible only when signed in to a MoneyFlow Cloud account with the admin email — the link doesn't even appear in the sidebar otherwise, and every action is also checked server-side, not just hidden client-side:

  • Dashboard: total users, active/pending subscriptions, estimated monthly recurring revenue
  • Users: suspend/activate accounts, change a user's plan, and manually activate a subscription until a live payment gateway is connected
  • Plans: create, edit, or deactivate subscription plans, pricing, and feature lists
  • Discounts: create percent/flat discount codes with redemption limits, expiry dates, and plan restrictions
Open Admin Portal
